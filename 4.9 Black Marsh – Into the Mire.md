
**Location Description:**  
The swampy expanse stretches endlessly before you, the wet ground sucking at your boots with every step. The air is thick with the stench of rot and stagnant water. Gnarled black trees lean in from every side, their roots curling like skeletal fingers above the marsh pools. Far-off, the croak of unseen creatures carries through the mist, and somewhere… a slow, rhythmic splashing — too deliberate to be an animal — echoes through the reeds.

---

### **Encounter – Marshlight Ambush** 💀 _(Optional)_

A pale green flame flickers ahead, floating just above the water’s surface. If the party follows it, it leads them deeper into the marsh until they realize the light is not a lantern — but a **Marshlight Wraith**, an incorporeal spirit that feeds on warmth and life.

As it approaches, three **Bogbound Corpses** claw their way from the mud to aid it.

**Tactical Note:**

- The Wraith attempts to lure the party into deeper water (difficult terrain).
    
- The corpses try to grapple and drag heroes under.
    
- Defeating the Wraith causes its light to burst, revealing a **submerged path of moss-covered stones** — safe passage deeper into the marsh.
    

---

### **Lore Discovery – Broken Zakarum Shield** 🛡️

On the mossy stone path, the heroes find **half of a cracked iron shield**, etched with the same crest they’ve seen on the Cathedral doors in Tristram.  
This relic hints at the Cathedral’s long, corrupt reach — even here in the Black Marsh.

---

### **Travel & Atmosphere** 🌫️

- Water deepens; the party must protect their gear.
    
- Reflections in the water occasionally twist into faces and shapes that _aren’t their own_.
    
- The air feels charged, as if the marsh itself is watching.
