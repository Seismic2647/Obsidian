**Location:** Meditation Room, The Broken Tower  
**Theme:** Elemental horror, memory-warped spirit  
**Trigger:** Touching the handprint, completing the phrase “Alvex,” or spending too long in the presence of the altar

> A thin crack splits across the stone wall. From within it, **roots slither out like worms**, wrapping around a **humanoid figure** — pallid, **skinned**, and dripping sap-colored ichor. It does not walk — it is **carried** by the vines, and its **eyeless face** turns toward Azura as if it _remembers her_. It opens a mouth filled with curling tendrils and **speaks her name backwards**.  
> _"Eruza..."_

**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened

**Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

---

### Traits

**Vinebound Body.**  
At the start of each of its turns, the Root-Priest regains 3 HP if touching earth or stone. Fire damage prevents regeneration on the following turn.

**Echoform.**  
This entity is **not fully real**. It can move through cracks, roots, or crevices no larger than 1 inch. It can't pass through walls or solid objects but can appear from any unsealed vine network.

**Mind-Scratch.**  
When a creature ends its turn within 10 feet, it must succeed a **DC 13 Wisdom save** or suffer **1d6 psychic damage** and disadvantage on its next attack roll.

---

### Actions

**Tendril Lash (Melee Weapon Attack).**  
+4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 8 (2d6 + 1) slashing damage. Target must succeed on a **DC 13 Strength save** or be **restrained by root-tendrils** (escape DC 14 on subsequent turns).

**Wither Breath (Recharge 5–6).**  
The Echo exhales a **cone of gray spores** in a 15-ft. cone. Each creature in that area must make a **DC 14 Constitution save** or take **3d8 necrotic damage** and **cannot regain HP** until the end of its next turn. On a success, half damage and no penalty.

**Speak the Seed (1/day).**  
The Echo whispers a secret to one creature that can hear it within 60 feet. That creature must make a **DC 13 Charisma saving throw**. On a fail, it is **charmed for 1 minute**, believes the Echo is a **forgotten mentor**, and **will not willingly attack it**. Save ends at the end of each turn.
