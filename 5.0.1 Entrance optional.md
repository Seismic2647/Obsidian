## **Hall of Echoes – Interactive Elements**

### 1. **The Cracked Saint**

- **Description:** One of the statues along the left wall depicts a hooded saint clutching a book to its chest. The stone is split from shoulder to hip, and a faint **cold mist leaks from the crack**.
    
- **Investigation:** A DC 13 Arcana check reveals the book’s carved runes are wards against possession — but they’ve been **deliberately defaced** centuries ago. Touching the statue causes a fleeting vision: the saint screaming as shadowed hands pull her into darkness.
    
- **Foreshadowing:** Suggests that the Cathedral’s sanctity has been deliberately dismantled by someone (or something) long before the party arrived.
    

---

### 2. **The Brazier’s Whisper**

- **Description:** The huge unlit brazier on the dais is blackened, its edges dented and warped. When a player places their hand near it, they hear a faint **whisper in an unknown tongue**.
    
- **Investigation:** A DC 15 Religion check identifies the language as an ancient angelic dialect. It repeats the phrase:
    
    > “Light the way, and face what the light reveals.”
    
- **Foreshadowing:** Implies the Cathedral holds something that fears light — and that the brazier may play a role later.
    

---

### 3. **The Blood-Red Window**

- **Description:** Above the dais, a tall stained-glass window shows a winged warrior battling a horned demon. The moonlight shining through turns the glass **blood-red**.
    
- **Investigation:** A DC 14 History check recalls a local legend — the “Siege of the Black Marsh,” when an angelic champion supposedly struck down a demon lord here.
    
- **Foreshadowing:** The broken lower panes reveal the **stone wall behind is scarred and clawed**, suggesting something huge once burst _into_ this chamber, not out of it.
