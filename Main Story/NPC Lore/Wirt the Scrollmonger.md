**Wirt the Scrollmonger**  
Once a sharp-eyed street urchin in the alleys of Tristram, Wirt made his living by picking locks, pockets, and occasionally the wrong target. One night, he lifted a silver-bound tome from a hooded traveler at the edge of town. Turned out the book was cursed — and the traveler wasn’t human. Wirt vanished for three days. When he returned, his right leg was gone below the knee, replaced with a crude wooden peg. He never spoke of what happened in those missing hours, only that “the price of forbidden knowledge is always paid in flesh.”

Now he sells scrolls and tomes from a crooked little stall near the well, his goods ranging from harmless firestarters to ancient incantations that seem to whisper when unrolled. Some say he’s still looking for that first cursed book — hoping to return it... or finish what he started.
