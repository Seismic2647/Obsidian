## **First Chamber – The Hall of Echoes**

The heavy doors open inward with a long, tortured groan, revealing a **vast and hollow nave**. The ceiling arches high above like the ribcage of some titanic creature, its beams wrapped in dangling cobwebs thick as rope. Dim shafts of moonlight pierce the broken stained-glass windows, splashing fractured colors onto the dusty flagstones.

Every step you take **echoes unnaturally** — too loud, too long, as though a hundred invisible feet are following in your wake. Stone statues of saints and warriors line the walls, their faces eaten away by time, their eyes replaced with deep hollows that seem to track your movements.

A **long crimson carpet**, faded to brown in places, stretches from the doorway to a raised dais at the far end. On the dais sits a **massive iron brazier**, unlit, but still faintly warm to the touch — as if it remembers the fires that once roared within it.

Somewhere deep in the cathedral, you hear a faint **drip… drip… drip**, followed by a sound that might be wind… or might be whispering.
