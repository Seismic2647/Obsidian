## **Transition: Into the Cathedral’s Heart**

As the last echo of their footsteps dies in the Hall, the air changes.  
The heavy wooden doors ahead — massive things bound in blackened iron — are already **slightly ajar**. A faint, rhythmic sound drifts through the gap… like **breathing**.

When the party approaches:

- The light from the Hall of Echoes does not reach far beyond the doorway. Instead, the darkness inside feels _thicker_, as if it resists their torches.
    
- Cold air seeps out, carrying the stale scent of incense, rot, and damp stone.
    

The moment the first character steps through:

- A sudden **gust of wind** slams the doors behind them with a booming crash, sealing the Hall of Echoes away.
    
- For a heartbeat, they swear they hear **something enormous shifting in the darkness ahead**.
    
- Then… silence.
    

---

**They’ve entered the Nave of Shadows** — a vast, open chamber with rows of crumbling pews, broken statuary, and a ceiling lost in darkness. Thin threads of moonlight spill through cracks in the roof, illuminating only tiny patches of the floor. In those pale spots, dust drifts lazily… except for one, where the dust has been **disturbed by fresh, clawed footprints** leading deeper into the Cathedral.
