## **Black Marsh – The Weeping Gate** 🕯️

**Location Description:**  
Through the thinning mist, the party finds a causeway of broken stone leading to an enormous, half-sunken gatehouse. Two blackened statues of kneeling knights flank the arch, their faces eroded by centuries of swamp rain. A slow trickle of water seeps from the gate’s hinges, dark and tinged red. The air is unnaturally still — even the frogs and insects have fallen silent.

---

### **Event – The Gate’s Warden** ⚔️ _(Mandatory)_

As the party approaches, the swamp beneath the gate begins to bubble and churn. Rising from the mire is **The Hollow Knight**, a spectral guardian bound in rusted armor, his chestplate caved in where a mortal blow once struck him down.  
His voice is a hollow echo:

> _“The Cathedral is sealed to the living. Pass only if you are willing to pay in pain.”_

The Warden demands proof of the party’s worth — not through tribute, but through combat.

---

**Combat Notes:**

- The Hollow Knight fights with a long, spectral halberd (reach 10 ft).
    
- Strikes drain stamina (reduce movement by 5 ft on hit, stacking).
    
- The marshwater around the gate is difficult terrain — clever players may lure him into deeper patches to slow him further.
    

**Victory:**  
Defeating the Hollow Knight causes his armor to collapse into the mud, revealing a skeletal hand clutching a **Rust-pitted Key**.  
This key fits a lock within the Cathedral’s outer doors.

---

### **Lore Tie-In** 📜

If examined, the Hollow Knight’s armor bears the same Zakarum crest as the Broken Shield found earlier in the marsh, tying both encounters together. This suggests that the Cathedral’s corruption is ancient, and its reach once extended into every corner of this land.
